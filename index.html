ABOUT US

EFA Nyagahanga Technical Secondary School
is a vocational school established with the aim
of providing practical knowledge that helps
young people to become self-reliant.
Our goal is to develop students with knowledge,
skills, and values that build a bright future for them.

To improve the quality and modernization of Technical
and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) in
Rwanda by building strong relationships
with stakeholders and providing training on modern
best practices.

To equip Rwanda's youth with the practical skills
and tools needed to become skilled and
compassionate leaders, by providing globally
competitive and high-quality TVET education.

Our Services

At EFA Nyagahanga Technical Secondary School, we offer high-quality technical and vocational education in the following combinations:

Software Development

We train students to design, code, and maintain computer applications and websites.

Food & Beverage

We equip learners with catering, nutrition, and hospitality skills for the service industry.

Agriculture

We train modern farming techniques for improved production and sustainability.

Nursing

We provide essential healthcare and first aid training for future medical professionals.

Electronics

We prepare students to work with electrical systems and modern electronic devices.

Land Survey

We teach accurate mapping, measurement, and land management skills.

Plumbing

We provide training in water system installation and maintenance.

Animal Health

Learners gain skills in animal care, disease control, and livestock management to support healthy and productive animals.
